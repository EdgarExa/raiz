## 3. Top 3 Detected Friction Points

1. **Fear of breaking the phone or deleting data:**  
   There is an initial hesitation before touching the screen, stemming from a lack of experience with apps outside of WhatsApp. Users fear taking an irreversible action.

2. **Reading difficulty under direct sunlight and text legibility:**  
   Small typography and medium contrast become unreadable in open field conditions, combined with the fact that several older producers require reading glasses.

3. **Conceptual confusion with institutional terminology and iconography:**  
   The official seal and technical terms (e.g., "Digital Passport", "Batch", "Verification") raised initial suspicion of being a bureaucratic process or government tax rather than a guarantee of authenticity.

---

## 4. Immediate UX Improvement Proposal

1. **Enlarged buttons and accessible interface (High Contrast & Permanent Microphone):**  
   * Design a prominent recording button (minimum 64x64 px) in a high-contrast green or yellow color.
   * Allow single-tap interaction to start/stop (with a visible sound wave indicator) so users are not forced to hold the button down.

2. **Field visual optimization (Outdoor Mode):**  
   * Increase the base font size to a minimum of 18 pt for headings and 16 pt for body text.
   * Use maximum contrast backgrounds (black text on white or light yellow background) to mitigate sun glare.

3. **Language redesign and contextualization of the Digital Passport:**  
   * Replace technical terms with clear local concepts: change *"Digital Passport"* to *"Pure Honey and Origin Certificate"*.
   * Add an explicit tagline under the TecNM seal: *"University Technical Backing - 100% Pure Honey Guarantee"*, avoiding shields that resemble tax or revenue seals.
