## 🎯 1. Activity Purpose & Expected Student Deliverable

Having already validated rural artisan ergonomics (in San Pablo Tijaltepec, San José Xochixtlán, San Juan Mixtepec, and Chalcatongo), TecNM engineering students must now validate the **buyer side of the equation**:
1. **Market & Ethical Willingness-to-Pay:** Understand what ethical consumers, specialty café owners, baristas, and collectors value in Mixtec origin products, and whether verifiable provenance justifies an ethical price premium.
2. **Yucu Direct Logistics Model:** Validate that buyers clearly understand that **Yucu is NOT a delivery app or middleman platform**. Yucu charges zero commissions and operates zero delivery trucks. The buyer and the artisan/cooperative negotiate delivery and payment directly.
3. **Indigenous Privacy & Sovereignty:** Evaluate how buyers react to the 3 artisan visibility levels (Full Name, First Name + Community, or Cooperative Name) and to certificates under private custody with an active Stellar cryptographic stamp.
4. **UX Usability Testing:** Measure real-world ergonomics of the Digital Passport (QR scanning speed, comprehension of the Zero-PII Stellar proof, and direct contact flow).

## 📋 2. What the Student Must Do Before Going to the Field

### Required Equipment:
1. **Smartphone with mobile data or offline capability:** Open the Yucu web application or a product passport demo.
2. **Physical QR Code Hang-Tag:** Print or display a sample QR code generated by the Yucu Hang-Tag modal.
3. **Stopwatch / Timer App:** To record time-on-task during the 5 usability exercises.
4. **Notepad / Voice Recorder:** To capture exact quotes (*Voice of the Customer*) upon verbal consent.

### Where to Find Real Buyers:
* Local specialty coffee shops in Tlaxiaco, Oaxaca City, Huajuapan, or Nochixtlán.
* Organic markets and fair-trade craft exhibitions (*Mercado El Pochote*, agricultural fairs).
* Baristas, coffee roasters, and textile collectors.
* Ethical tourists or consumers seeking authentic regional origin.

## 🎙️ 3. Step-by-Step Student Protocol During the Interview

### Step 1: Professional Introduction & Informed Consent (1 minute)
> *"Hello, good afternoon. We are Computer Systems Engineering students from the Technological Institute of Tlaxiaco (TecNM). We are developing an open-source community traceability protocol called Yucu Mixteca to support local coffee growers and artisans. We would appreciate 8 minutes of your time to ask about your buying habits and test a screen on our phone. May we take notes for our university research report?"*

### Step 2: Qualitative Discovery Questions (3 to 4 minutes)
Ask the questions in **Part 3** of the template:
1. What motivates you to buy authentic Mixtec origin products over industrial alternatives?
2. Have you ever doubted authenticity or encountered counterfeit items?
3. What is your reaction to commercial plagiarism of indigenous Mixtec designs?
4. **Explain Stellar Zero-PII:** *"Yucu records an immutable cryptographic fingerprint on the Stellar blockchain proving the piece was certified in its community, but keeps the artisan's personal data off-chain to protect her sovereignty."* Does this give you confidence?
5. **Explain Direct Logistics:** *"Yucu does not operate delivery trucks or charge shipping fees; it connects you directly via WhatsApp with the artisan to agree on local pickup or courier shipping."* How do you evaluate this?
6. **Artisan Visibility:** What do you think about artisans choosing to display only their first name or cooperative name for privacy protection?

### Step 3: Usability Testing Protocol (Think-Aloud Protocol) (3 to 4 minutes)
Hand the smartphone with the QR tag to the buyer:
> *"Now I will hand you this tag and the phone. Please perform the 5 tasks I will announce, and tell me out loud what you are thinking, seeing, and feeling. If something is confusing, remember that you are never at fault—our software design is what is being evaluated."*

- **Task 1:** Scan the QR hang-tag and load the Digital Passport.
- **Task 2:** Find who made the piece, their community, and hours invested.
- **Task 3:** Inspect the Stellar Blockchain authenticity certificate.
- **Task 4:** Find how to contact the artisan directly to arrange delivery.
- **Task 5:** Browse the community showcase by product category or town.

### Step 4: Standardized System Usability Scale (SUS) (2 minutes)
Ask the buyer to rate the 10 statements from 1 (*Strongly Disagree*) to 5 (*Strongly Agree*).

---

## 🧮 4. Calculating the System Usability Scale (SUS) Score

- **Odd items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9):** `Item Score = User Rating - 1`
- **Even items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10):** `Item Score = 5 - User Rating`
- **Total SUS Score:** `(Sum of all 10 item scores) * 2.5` (Resulting in a score from 0 to 100).
- **Scale:**
  - **85 - 100:** Grade A+ (Exceptional usability)
  - **70 - 84:** Grade B (Good / Acceptable)
  - **50 - 69:** Grade C/D (Marginal / Requires UX refactoring)
  - **< 50:** Grade F (Unacceptable / High friction)
